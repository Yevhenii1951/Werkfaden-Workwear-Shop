# Промпты для генерации изображений (English)

## Общие правила для всех промптов

- **Style:** Editorial photography, honest craftsmanship aesthetic, medium format film look.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffused daylight, no harsh shadows.
- **Color Palette:** Muted charcoal, warm paper tones, with brick-orange accents.
- **Background:** Neutral light grey or authentic, clean workshop environments.
- **Restrictions:** NO text, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO fake safety certificates (EN/ISO), NO dangerous work scenarios, NO depiction of regular clothing as certified PPE.

## Product Photography (Catalog)

**Template:** "Editorial product photography, a [COLOR] [PRODUCT TYPE] (e.g., navy work t-shirt, anthracite softshell jacket) displayed on a neutral light grey background. Soft diffused daylight, medium format film aesthetic. Clearly visible stitching, fabric texture, and accurate cut. Clean composition, aspect ratio 4:5. No text, no logos, no watermarks."

**Color Mapping:**

- Schwarz: pure black fabric, subtle texture.
- Marineblau: deep navy blue, accurate hex #172B46.
- Anthrazit: dark grey, matte finish.
- Weiß: off-white, natural cotton tone, not blown out.
- Oliv: muted olive green, accurate hex #556B2F.

## Lifestyle Photography

**Template:** "Editorial lifestyle photography, a [AGE/GENDER] person wearing [SPECIFIC CLOTHING ITEMS], engaged in [SAFE, REALISTIC TASK, e.g., reviewing blueprints, light gardening, painting a wall]. Soft diffused daylight, medium format film aesthetic, muted charcoal and warm paper tones with brick-orange accent. Authentic expression of [FOCUS/ENJOYMENT/CRAFTSMANSHIP]. Clean composition, aspect ratio 4:5. No text, no logos."

**Subjects:** Diverse representation (e.g., 70% men aged 35-55, some 25-30 and 55+, at least two women, e.g., gardener, painter). Show specific actions and tools (e.g., electrician's hands with wiring, installing copper heating pipes, painting with a roller).
