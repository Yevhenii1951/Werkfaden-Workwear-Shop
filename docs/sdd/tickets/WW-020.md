# WW-020: A listing shows what the shopper can actually buy, from the whole catalog

Status: in progress (branch `fix/ww-020-listing-correctness`).

Surface: `S` (storefront data path and filter util).

## Intent / delivers

Closes the listing findings of the 2026-10-25 review.

**S2a — price and stock were checked on different variants.** `productMatchesFilters`
took the cheapest and the most expensive variant of a product and compared that
span with the price range, then asked separately whether *any* variant was in
stock. A product with a 10 € (sold out) and a 100 € (in stock) variant therefore
matched "50–60 €, in stock" and put a card on the page that cannot be bought in
that range. Price and availability are variant facts, so one variant now has to
satisfy the price range *and* the stock filter (`variantMatchesListing`).

**S2b — the listing stopped at 100 products.** `listProductsWithSort` fetched
one page of 100 and filtered, sorted and paginated that array, so products
101+ were unreachable and the page count came from a filtered array. It now
walks every page (`LISTING_PAGE_SIZE = 100`) and derives `count` from the
filtered result. A guard throws a readable error above
`MAX_LISTING_PRODUCTS = 5_000`: that catalog needs server-side filtering, not a
longer loop.

**S2c — `nextPage` was an offset.** `listProductsWithSort` returned
`(page - 1) * limit + limit` while `listProducts` and the pagination component
speak page numbers. No caller reads it today; it now matches the rest of the
data layer.

Nothing. The filters themselves arrived with WW-002/FR-12.

## Context to read

This ticket + ../contract.md (FR-12 catalog filters) + ../state.md. Store API
facts used: `/store/products` is offset/limit only, `count` is the total, and
`*variants.calculated_price` is the priced variant list — check the installed
`@medusajs/medusa` 2.21.2 source rather than memory.

## Verification scenario

GIVEN a catalog larger than one page WHEN a shopper opens a category or
collection THEN every product is reachable through pagination and the page count
follows the filtered count; and GIVEN the filters "50–60 €" and "in stock" WHEN
a product only reaches that range with one variant and only has stock in another
THEN it is not listed.

## Acceptance criteria

- `variantMatchesListing` is the only place that decides price + availability;
  `productMatchesFilters` accepts a product when at least one variant matches.
- `listProductsWithSort` fetches every page; `count` is the filtered count;
  more than 5 000 products fails loudly instead of silently truncating.
- New unit spec `src/lib/data/__tests__/products-listing.unit.spec.ts`: a 130
  product catalog is fully reachable across 11 pages of 12, the filtered count
  is the filtered count, and a catalog of 25 needs exactly one request of
  `limit = 100`.
- `listing-filters.unit.spec.ts` covers the straddling-variant case and the
  "one variant must be priced *and* available" case.
- Existing storefront units, `npm run check:ui`, `npm run build:ui` and a full
  `npm run check` stay green.

## Non-goals

No server-side filtering in the backend, no search-module change, no new
filters, no redesign of the refinement UI.

## Known limits

- The demo catalog has 25 products with one price per product, so S2a and S2b
  are observable only through the specs above and a catalog above 100 products.
  Verified against the live API instead: 25 products, 24.90–89.90 €, every
  product has at least one variant in stock, and a category page with
  "50–60 €" correctly renders its own empty state (`data-testid="no-products"`)
  while an unfiltered one renders 5 cards.
- In-memory filtering means the storefront fetches the catalog per
  revalidation. Fine at 25–500 products with the `products` cache tag; a real
  catalog needs the search index to filter.
- The `/store` listing itself runs on the built-in Postgres search module
  (`POST /store/search` answers), not on this data path; the review's "no
  search module" note is stale. `docs/sdd/design.md` still lists it as open and
  is corrected in WW-023.
