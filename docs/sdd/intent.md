# Intent

Purpose: PORTFOLIO. Tier: Standard.

Build a demonstrably working German workwear shop for small teams and individuals. A buyer chooses size/color, checks availability, completes a sandbox purchase, and views their own orders. A shop operator maintains products and processes orders through Medusa Admin. A buyer can separately request logo personalization, which the operator reviews manually.

The project should show client-style delivery: coherent content, trustworthy totals, authorization, stock handling, recovery from payment failures, and reproducible setup. It remains a demo: no real merchant identity, live payments, real fulfillment or claims of legal launch readiness.

Working brand: Werkfaden, subject to name clearance for any commercial use. Store language de-DE, currency EUR, destination Germany. The initial catalog contains 25 products and 150 variants; generic fixture products may be used until approved content arrives.

First complete the functional store using the starter's existing design. Landing-page polish is a separate, later design intake. Do not add a second backend, CMS, ERP, POS or AI chatbot to the MVP.

User authorization: 2026-10-04 — PORTFOLIO selected, functioning client-style scenarios requested; SDD and setup authorized. Scope details below are the proposed baseline, not claims of completed features.
