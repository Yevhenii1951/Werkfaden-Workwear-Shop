# WW-018: Header navigation with service dropdown

Status: implemented on branch `feature/ww-018-header-navigation`; `npm run check`
+ build green, browser-verified at 1440×900 and 1024×800. Round 1 of the
read-only review is addressed (see "Checks / browser evidence"). Pending: fresh
read-only review, merge.

## Intent / delivers

The header offered three flat links ("Kollektion", "Alle Artikel", "Service").
The Kollektion panel was a 565 px band with crushed tiles, the services were
buried in the footer, and nothing signalled that a tab opens a panel.

This ticket rebuilds the header navigation:

- **Kollektion** opens a full-width mega menu (collections list + three
  product tiles in `aspect-[16/10]`, the ratio of the home collections grid).
  The band spans the whole viewport width (1425 px at 1440) instead of the
  width of the centred nav row.
- **Alle Artikel** stays a link and gets a hover/focus hint that points to the
  full catalog.
- **Leistungen** opens a dropdown with the three B2B services (Logo-Veredelung,
  Angebote, Stickerei), each with a one-line description and an info icon that
  shows a hint on hover and on keyboard focus. The hint opens next to the icon
  outside the dropdown, so it never covers the description of its own entry.
- Both dropdown tabs carry a chevron that rotates while the panel is open.
- The mobile side menu groups the same services under "Leistungen" and moves
  the legal pages into their own group "Rechtliches".
- The footer column "Service & Konto" becomes "Leistungen & Konto" and the
  link label "Stickerei-Preisrechner" becomes "Stickerei", so the wording
  matches the navigation.
- The three services are defined once in
  `apps/storefront/src/modules/layout/lib/service-links.ts`; the dropdown, the
  mobile side menu and the footer all read from it. The five collections live
  once in `lib/collection-links.ts` and are shared by the mega menu and the
  mobile side menu.

Behaviour: one `openMenu` state, so only one panel is open at a time; the panel
closes 200 ms after the pointer leaves it (the delay bridges the gap between
the tab and the panel). A tab opens its panel on click and on keyboard focus;
the click keeps an open panel open, the panel closes when the pointer leaves
the navigation row, when the focus leaves it or with Escape — Escape moves the
focus back to the tab. The Kollektion panel sits inside the "Kollektion" tab in
the DOM, so its links follow the tab in the tab order; "Alle Artikel" closes
an open panel, otherwise its hint would fall onto the tiles of the band. Both
panels stay in the DOM, so the `aria-controls` of a tab always points at an
existing element; the tile images are only fetched after the panel has been
opened once.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Kollektion panel spans the full viewport width; the tiles keep the
  `aspect-[16/10]` ratio and are clickable links.
- The Leistungen dropdown lists Logo-Veredelung, Angebote and Stickerei with a
  description each; the info icon exposes the hint via `aria-describedby`
  and shows it on hover and on focus.
- Exactly one panel is open at a time; `aria-expanded` on the tabs mirrors
  the state; the chevron rotates only for the open tab.
- The hint of an entry does not appear when the entry link itself is hovered,
  and it never covers the description of its own entry.
- The mobile side menu shows the groups "Leistungen" and "Rechtliches".
- The footer column is named "Leistungen & Konto", the embroidery link is
  labelled "Stickerei".
- Keyboard: tabs and hints are reachable and operable. Tabbing forward from
  the "Kollektion" tab reaches the collection links and the tiles of the open
  panel, the "Leistungen" tab opens its dropdown instead, and the hint of an
  entry opens on focus. A click opens the panel of a tab, Escape closes it and
  returns the focus to the tab.
- The three service entries are defined in one place and shared by the
  dropdown, the mobile side menu and the footer; the five collections likewise.
- `npm run check` green; build green; browser evidence in the PR.

## Non-goals

No search rework, no account-menu rework, no sticky/shrinking header, no
mega menu on mobile, no new pages.

## Known limits

- The mega menu is desktop-only (`hidden lg:block`); on mobile the same
  collections are reachable through the side menu and the footer.
- The hints explain the demo behaviour of the services (unbinding request,
  one-time discount code, estimate). They are static copy and are not
  generated from the backend.
- The hint of a service entry opens next to its icon, outside the dropdown
  panel. It therefore hangs over the page next to the panel instead of over
  the panel content.
- A click on a tab opens its panel but does not collapse an open panel again.
  The panel closes when the pointer leaves the navigation row, when the focus
  leaves it or with Escape.

  stays open; Escape inside the panel → the panel closes and the focus is back

  only after the first opening: on `/de/store` the page loads no mega menu
  image before the first opening (0 of 3) and all three after it.
- "Alle Artikel" closes an open panel, so its hint (y 124–204 px) no longer
  falls onto the tiles of the band.
- The description of the service "Angebote" now says "an der Kasse", like the
  quote page and the checkout ("Rabattcode(s) hinzufügen").
