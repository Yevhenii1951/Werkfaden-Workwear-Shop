# Tier

Purpose: PORTFOLIO — demonstrate a working German workwear store with client-style delivery and sandbox payments.

Tier: Standard.

Why: Real commerce scenarios and security evidence, without accepting real payments or presenting the demo as a trading business.

MVP budget: 10 vertical tickets. Landing-page redesign and advanced B2B automation are later phases.

Auth, uploads and payments require a security review before merge/release even at Standard. Production requires an explicit purpose/tier revision and a separate launch gate.
