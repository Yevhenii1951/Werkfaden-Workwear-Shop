# Content handoff

Give `llm-prompt-ru.md` to the content LLM. Return the resulting files here under `incoming/` for validation, not directly to application source or the database.

Priority: brand + demo policies + categories + one complete product first; full products/variants and images next; page/UI/email copy afterwards. Landing design is deferred.

Catalog: 25 products, 150 variants, EUR gross demo prices, Germany only. Machine keys and SKU must be stable. JSON/CSV is an intermediate content contract, not a native Medusa import.

Generated business/product facts remain fictional fixtures. No operator identity, reviews, safety certifications, commercial guarantees or legal launch claims. Legal documents are drafts/placeholders only.

Image prompts are not image assets; each actual asset needs provenance/license and matching image_key. Images and brand require separate approval before commercial use.
